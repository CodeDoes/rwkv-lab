# Sakura Academy of Hearts: A Romance & Slice-of-Life TTRPG Guide

## Section 1: Campaign Introduction and Philosophical Foundations

Welcome, Match Master (MM), to *Sakura Academy of Hearts*. This book is an incredibly detailed, comprehensive, deep-dive adventure guide designed to assist you in running a romance-focused, slice-of-life TTRPG campaign of school clubs, teenage drama, seasonal festivals, and high-stakes confessions. In this section, we will establish the foundations of Sakura Academy: its colorful, nostalgic aesthetic, its social mechanical frameworks, its core themes, and the grand three-act structure that will guide your players from their first nervous introductions to their final confessions under the legendary cherry blossoms.

### Setting the Scene: Sakura Academy
Sakura Academy is a picturesque high school located in a sleepy, sun-drenched coastal town. It is a world of gentle sea breezes, white sailor-collar uniforms, sprawling wooden corridors that smell of floor-wax, and massive cherry blossom trees that shower the courtyard in a continuous rain of soft pink petals.
* **The Nostalgic Aesthetic:** Think of golden hour sunlight streaming through classroom windows, cold melon soda cans pressed against warm cheeks, bicycle tires crunching on gravel paths, hand-written letters tucked into wooden shoe lockers, and the distant, rhythmic hum of cicadas in the summer afternoon.
* **The Slice-of-Life Sandbox:** Unlike traditional TTRPGs where players hunt monsters, the "combats" in Sakura Academy are social encounters. A player's "weapons" are their Wit, Charm, and Courage, and their "battlefield" is the school rooftop, the library archive, or the local arcade.
* **The Focus on Intimacy:** The goal of the game is not to save the world, but to form deep, meaningful connections. Players must navigate the fragile, often terrifying waters of teenage romance, managing social anxiety, friendship loyalties, and romantic rivals.

### Core Romance Themes
* **The Power of Vulnerability:** To win a love interest's heart, a player cannot just show off high stats; instead, they must show vulnerability. This means listening to their problems, sharing secrets during rainstorms, and supporting them through personal struggles.
* **Social Stress and High Stakes:** High school is a pressure cooker of rumors and expectations. Players must balance their study grades, club responsibilities, and part-time jobs while trying to find time for romance. If their Stress levels get too high, they risk burning out or suffering social embarrassment.
* **The Sweetness of the Mundane:** The most memorable scenes in a slice-of-life game are the quiet, everyday moments: walking home together in the rain under a shared umbrella, sharing a bento box on the roof, or studying together in a dusty corner of the library.

### Campaign Structure
* **Act I: The Spring of Introductions.** The players arrive at Sakura Academy as transfer students. They must choose their school clubs, establish their personal statistics, meet the primary love interests, and survive the chaotic club-recruitment week.
* **Act II: The Summer of Festivals.** The heat rises. The players navigate the summer vacation, planning beach trips, study sessions, and dates at the grand Summer Firework Festival, where romantic tensions reach a boiling point.
* **Act III: The Autumn of Confessions.** The climax of the school year. During the legendary Cultural Festival, the players must participate in the school play, navigate final romantic misunderstandings, and prepare to confess their love under the legendary Heart-Sakura Tree on graduation night.

## Section 2: Social Circles of the Academy

To successfully run this social sandbox, you must understand the primary cliques and clubs at Sakura Academy. These groups represent factions that offer social perks, resources, and access to specific locations and love interests.

```
                 =============================================
                 |     THE SOCIAL CIRCLES OF SAKURA ACADEMY  |
                 =============================================
                                      / \
                                     /   \
                                    /     \
                                   /       \
                     [The Student Council Elite] \
                     (Order / Authority / Pride)  \
                               /               \
                              /                 \
                             /                   \
                            /                     \
       [The Sports Club Alliance] <-------------> [The Occult Research Club]
       (Athletic / High Energy)                   (Social Outcasts / Mystery)
```

### 1. The Student Council Elite
* **Overview:** The pristine, high-achieving student government who run the academy with administrative efficiency. They control school funding, event approvals, and disciplinary actions. They value discipline, academic excellence, and public reputation.
* **Key Figures:**
  * *Council President Miyu Vance:* A brilliant, unyielding girl who wears her glasses with pride and demands perfection from every student. She views romance as a distraction from academic duty.
* **Methods:** Disciplinary warnings, hall patrols, student council meetings, and control over school event planning.
* **Attitude toward PCs:** Strict and suspicious. If the PCs are caught skipping class or causing drama in the halls, Miyu will sentence them to library cleanup duty. If they join the council and maintain high grades, she can become a powerful, though tsundere, ally.

### 2. The Sports Club Alliance (The Jocks)
* **Overview:** The high-energy, popular athletic clubs, dominated by the swim, soccer, and track teams. They are the social royalty of the school, popular with the student body and funded heavily by local sports brands.
* **Key Figures:**
  * *Ryota "The Comet" Stone:* The charismatic captain of the soccer team, who is incredibly athletic but dense when it comes to academic studies and romantic cues.
* **Methods:** Morning practice drills, sports tournaments, loud pep rallies, and after-school protein shakes.
* **Attitude toward PCs:** Warm and welcoming, provided the PCs can keep up. If the PCs join a sports club, they gain bonuses to their Courage and Stamina stats but must spend their afternoons practicing in the hot sun.

### 3. The Occult Research Club (The Outcasts)
* **Overview:** A tiny, unofficial club that meets in a dusty, spider-webbed room in the old school building. They study local town legends, ghost stories, and perform mock tarot readings. They are viewed as weird eccentrics by the rest of the school.
* **Key Figures:**
  * *Yuki "Shadow-Born" Chen:* A quiet, gothic-dressed girl who always sits in the shadows of the library. She claims she can read the "aura of love" around people.
* **Methods:** Candlelit séances, ghost-hunting tours of the school at night, and rare book translation.
* **Attitude toward PCs:** Curious and slightly eerie. They will welcome the PCs if they display an interest in the supernatural or bring them rare old books. Joining this club gives bonuses to Wit but reduces general popularity with the popular cliques.

## Section 3: Romance Mechanics and Character Dashboards

To run *Sakura Academy of Hearts* effectively, you must track the players' personal statistics, their affection levels with love interests, and their social stress. Use the following character dashboard template:

```

                      |       SAKURA ACADEMY STUDENT LOG       |
                      =========================================
                      | Courage: [||||||    ] Tier 2 (Decisive)|
                      | Charm:   [|||||     ] Tier 1 (Cute)    |
                      | Wit:     [||||||||  ] Tier 3 (Brilliant)|
                      | Social Stress: [|||       ] 30%        |
                      | -------------------------------------- |
                      | Love Interest: Miyu Vance (Affection) |
                      | Heart Points: [|||||||   ] 70/100      |
                      | Relationship Status: "Nervous Crush"   |
                      =========================================
```

### Personal Statistics and Upgrades

#### 1. Courage
* **Description:** Represents the player's ability to speak their mind, stand up to bullies, and initiate romantic actions.
* **Tiers:**
  * *Tier 1 (Shy):* Cannot initiate a confession or ask a love interest out on a private date without stuttering.
  * *Tier 2 (Decisive):* Can confidently ask people out and stand up to rivals.
  * *Tier 3 (Bold):* Can perform heroic, public gestures of affection (e.g., confessing over the school intercom).

#### 2. Charm
* **Description:** Represents the player's physical appearance, fashion sense, and social grace.
* **Tiers:**
  * *Tier 1 (Average):* Standard student uniform. Gifts given are received with polite gratitude.
  * *Tier 2 (Stylish):* Customizes their uniform, wears trendy accessories. Love interests gain +5 extra Affection from all gifts.
  * *Tier 3 (Magnetic):* Turns heads in the hallway. Random students whisper when they pass, and love interests are easily flustered.

#### 3. Wit
* **Description:** Represents academic intelligence, wit during debates, and ability to notice subtle social cues.
* **Tiers:**
  * *Tier 1 (Oblivious):* Often misses romantic hints or sarcasms. High risk of failing exams.
  * *Tier 2 (Perceptive):* Can read body language, unlocking secret dialogue options during dates.
  * *Tier 3 (Genius):* Tops the school exam board, gaining immense respect from teachers and the Student Council.

### Social Stress and Heart Points

#### Social Stress System
* **How it grows:** Studing late for exams, participating in grueling sports practices, having awkward conversations with rivals, or being rejected.
* **The Consequences:** If Stress reaches 100%, the player suffers a "Social Breakdown." They cannot attend school for 2 days, reducing their study time, and their affection levels with all love interests drop by 15% due to missed connections.

#### Heart Points (Affection)
* **How it grows:** Giving preferred gifts, having successful dates, defending them from rumors, or spending quiet moments together.
* **The Confession Requirement:** To successfully confess to a love interest in Act III, the player must have accumulated at least 90 Heart Points with them.

## Section 4: Act I — The Spring of Introductions

The campaign opens on the first day of the Spring Semester. The air is warm, and the cherry blossoms are in full bloom, covering the stone steps of Sakura Academy in pink snow. The players must find their classrooms, choose their clubs, and survive their first social encounters.

### The Hook: The Umbrella Incident
On the third day of class, a sudden, torrential spring thunderstorm hits the town right as the final bell rings. The players find themselves stuck at the school shoe lockers, looking out at the heavy rain. Standing next to them is Miyu Vance, the Student Council President, who has forgotten her umbrella and is quietly shivering in her thin sailor-uniform.

#### Narration Block for the Umbrella Incident:
> "The sky over the coastal hills has turned a dark, bruised violet, and the rain is coming down in a thick, roaring sheet of silver water that bounces off the gravel courtyard. The cool sea breeze smells of ozone and wet pine needles. You stand at the wooden shoe lockers, unlatching your locker to retrieve your umbrella. Just next to you, hugging her books tight to her chest to keep them dry, is Miyu Vance. Her glasses are slightly fogged from the humidity, and her sailor-collar uniform is damp at the shoulders. She looks out at the storm, her lips parted in a tiny, frustrated sigh, before she glances at you. She quickly turns her head away, her cheeks flushing a soft pink, as she mutters, 'I... I didn't check the forecast this morning. What a stupid mistake...'"

### Resolving the Umbrella Crisis
The players must decide how to handle this quiet, high-stakes romantic opportunity:

#### 1. The Bold Move (The Shared Umbrella)
If the player has Tier 2 Courage, they can offer to share their umbrella and walk Miyu home. This requires a Courage check.
* **Systemic Result:** If successful, Miyu Vance gains +15 Heart Points. During the walk home, they share a quiet conversation about her strict family expectations, unlocking her personal questline.

#### 2. The Polite Move (The Umbrella Gift)
The player can hand their umbrella to Miyu, choosing to walk home in the rain themselves.
* **Systemic Result:** Miyu gains +10 Heart Points and views the player as a true gentleman. However, the player's Stress level increases by 20%, and they catch a cold, giving them disadvantage on physical checks for the next 2 days.

#### 3. The Oblivious Move (Skipping in the Rain)
The player ignores her, pops their umbrella, and skips out of the courtyard.
* **Systemic Result:** Miyu's affection level drops by 5, and she views the player as an insensitive slacker. However, the player's Stress is reduced to 0%.

## Section 5: Act II — The Summer of Festivals (The Date Sandbox)

As the summer vacation begins, the players enter an open-world sandbox phase where they must manage their calendar to plan dates, study sessions, and part-time jobs.

### Calendar Sandbox Navigation
The summer lasts for 4 Weeks (28 Days). Each day is split into three phases: *Morning*, *Afternoon*, and *Evening*. Players can choose one activity per phase:

```

                      =========================================

                      | Afternoon: Beach Date with Ryota        |
                      | Evening:   Study for Exams (-10% Stress)|
                      =========================================
```

### Key Act II Events and Date Locations
1. **The Coastal Beach Date:** The classic beach trip. The players can invite their crush to the beach to swim, eat shaved ice, and build sandcastles. Requires choosing a stylish swimsuit (costs 8000 yen at the mall) to boost Charm.
2. **The Library Study Session:** A quiet date in the archives. Spending an afternoon studying together increases Wit by 10 and reduces the love interest's exam anxiety, boosting Affection by +10.
3. **The Summer Firework Festival (Day 25):** The climax of the summer. The players must invite their primary crush to the festival, wear traditional yukatas, and attempt to hold hands during the grand finale fireworks show.

#### Narration Block for the Summer Beach Date:
> "The sand under your bare feet is white and burning hot, reflecting the brilliant, white-gold glare of the summer sun. ## Section 6: Love Interests and Major Non-Player Characters (NPCs)

To make your school romance rich, sweet, and engaging, here are the detailed dossiers of the primary love interests, complete with likes, dislikes, conversation guides, and secrets.

### 1. Miyu Vance (The Tsundere Student Council President)
* **Description:** A beautiful girl in her second year, with long black hair tied in a pristine ponytail and sharp, blue-framed glasses. She wears her school uniform perfectly without any alterations.
* **Voice and Mannerisms:** Speaks in a sharp, formal, high-class tone. She crosses her arms when she is flustered and has a habit of pushing up her glasses by the middle bridge when she is nervous or lying.
* **Likes and Dislikes:** Likes classical music, sweet lemon tea, rare fountain pens, and late-night study sessions. Dislikes lazy students, horror movies, and spicy food.
* **The Secret:** Miyu secretly loves cute mascot characters. She carries a small, fluffy pink rabbit plushie hidden inside her leather school bag, and if anyone discovers it, her cheeks will burn with absolute embarrassment.
* **Conversation Guide:** To win her heart, the player must focus on academics and support her student council duties. Avoid making flippant jokes or skipping class, as this will immediately lower her Affection.

### 2. Ryota Stone (The Dense Soccer Captain)
* **Description:** A tall, muscular boy with messy golden-brown hair, a wide grin, and a permanent tan. He wears his school uniform loosely with the top two buttons undone and a soccer ball tucked under his arm.
* **Voice and Mannerisms:** Speaks in a loud, boisterous, friendly laugh. He gestures wildly with his hands and is prone to giving bone-crushing high-fives or slaps on the back when he is excited.
* **Likes and Dislikes:** Likes sports cars, cold melon soda, pork cutlets, and early morning runs. Dislikes mathematics, romantic poetry, and rainstorms that cancel outdoor practices.
* **The Secret:** Ryota is secretly terrified of ghosts and darkness. If he is forced to enter the old school building at night or go through a haunted house display, he will scream and clutch onto the closest person for safety.
* **Conversation Guide:** To win his heart, the player must show high-energy enthusiasm for his soccer matches. Do not use overly complex vocabulary or double-meanings, as he will completely miss the romantic cues.

### The School Play: Romeo and Juliet
The players' class has chosen to put on a theater production of *Romeo and Juliet*. Based on their choices in Act II, the player has been cast as Romeo, and their primary crush is Juliet.
* **The Challenge:** During the live performance on Day 3, the player must deliver their lines with emotion, passion, and flawless execution. This requires a series of Wit and Charm checks.

## Section 8: Narrative Choice and Campaign Resolution

The final scene of *Sakura Academy of Hearts* is the players' confession. Based on their choices, their personal statistics, and their accumulated Heart Points, the campaign will resolve into one of the three primary paths.

### The Three Paths

```
                             ===========================
                             | THE CLIMATIC DECISION   |
                             ===========================
                                          |
         -------------------------------------------------------------------
         |                                |                                |
         v                                v                                v
===========================  ===========================  ===========================
|  PATH A: PERFECT ROMANCE |  |  PATH B: LIFELONG BESTIE|  |  PATH C: COLD SOLITUDE  |
|  - Confession accepted   |  |  - Romance fails but    |  |  - Both refuse PC       |
|  - walk home together    |  |    friendship secured   |  |  - Lonely study ending  |
|  - Graduation couple     |  |  - Club stays together  |  |  - No dates, high stress|
===========================  ===========================  ===========================
```

#### Path A: The Perfect Romance (The Confession Accepted)
* **The Action:** The player successfully confesses to Miyu or Ryota with over 90 Heart Points.
* **The Consequences:** The love interest blushes, cries, and accepts the confession, gifting the player their school button (a traditional sign of love).
* **The Epilogue:** The couple graduates from Sakura Academy together. They walk home through the cherry blossoms hand-in-hand. During the credits scene, we see polaroid photos of their college life together, proving that the legend of the Heart-Sakura was true.

#### Path B: The Bitter-Sweet Friendship (The Lifelong Bestie)
* **The Action:** The player confesses but has between 50 and 89 Heart Points.
* **The Consequences:** The love interest is deeply touched but admits they only see the player as a very dear, irreplaceable friend.
* **The Epilogue:** The player's confession is rejected, but they manage to preserve their close friendship. They walk home together one last time as classmates, promising to keep in touch during college. The club stays together, and though there is a lingering, sweet sadness, they are glad they met.

#### Path C: The Lonely Slacker (The Dreaded Study Ending)
* **The Action:** The player has high Stress, failed exams, or has less than 50 Heart Points with everyone.
* **The Consequences:** The player does not confess to anyone, spending graduation night alone on the school roof, looking out at the happy couples below.
* **The Epilogue:** The player graduates alone. They spend their spring vacation studying late at cram-school to make up for their failed exams, their only companion being a cold can of melon soda. They have solved no mysteries, won no championships, and remain single—a true slice-of-life slacker ending.

## Section 9: Match Master (MM) Advice and Troubleshooting

Running a slice-of-life romance campaign requires a delicate touch, excellent emotional pacing, and authentic roleplaying. Here is practical advice for dealing with common issues that may arise at your table.

### Facilitating Authentic Romance
Many players are used to combat-focused games and can feel awkward or silly roleplaying romantic interest in a tabletop setting. To handle this:
* **No Pressure:** Never force a player to speak in an authentic, dramatic voice if they feel uncomfortable. Let them describe their actions in the third person: "My character flushes, stutters a bit, and hands her the melon soda."
* **Keep it Sweet and PG:** Sakura Academy is a clean, classic school romance. Focus on emotional intimacy, hand-holding, and cute stuttered conversations. Keep the tone light, sweet, and focused on innocent teenage drama.
* **Use the Music:** Background music is your most powerful tool. Play gentle acoustic guitar, soft piano, or high-energy anime J-Pop during school scenes to establish the mood instantly.

### Managing Social Conflicts
Romantic games can sometimes lead to jealousy or disagreements between player characters if they are chasing the same love interest.
* **The Fair-Play Rule:** Establish during Session Zero that the love interests are independent characters who make their own choices based on who treats them best.
* **The Romantic Rival Duel:** If two PCs are chasing the same love interest, do not let them fight physically. Instead, have them engage in a "Social Duel" during the school sports day or exams to see who can impress the crush more, resolving the conflict in a fun, cinematic, and fair way.

This concludes the Dating-Sim Adventure Guide. May your cherry blossoms always fall at the perfect cinematic moment, your dates go flawlessly, and your confessions end in absolute, sweet success!
